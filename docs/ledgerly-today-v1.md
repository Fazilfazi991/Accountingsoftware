# Ledgerly Today V1 — read-only data contract

Today is a business-owner operating view at `/today`. It does not change Home, Dashboard, posting, schema, or navigation. Every request uses the authenticated cookie client and `requireOrganizationContext()`, passes the selected organization and branch to existing member-checked RPCs, and uses organization/branch/status filters on document queries. No service role is used. The organization's currency and timezone control display/date grouping.

| Figure | Existing source | Status/date semantics | Formula |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash now | `get_live_dashboard` cash_on_hand + cash_at_bank | journal entries `posted`/`reversed`, selected branch, active cash/bank accounts; includes reversal journal effects | Sum the two dashboard balances, never invoice amounts |
| Coming 7d | `get_open_item_report(receivable, open, as_of=today, branch)` | open/partial, positive outstanding, due date today through today+7 | Sum `outstanding` on due items, not original invoice amount or received cash |
| Going 7d | same report, `payable` | same due window | Sum remaining payable amounts, not supplier payment amounts |
| Simple projection | figures above | not a forecast; ignores undated, overdue and not-yet-posted items | cash now + coming − going |
| Outstanding customer invoices | `get_live_dashboard.receivables` | open-items on selected source journal branch with positive remaining | Exact posted/open-item RPC total |
| Overdue portion / attention | receivable/payable open-item reports | due date strictly before today; open items generated by source posting, with current `open`/`partial` status | Sum outstanding; actionable per-document rows |
| Received today | `customer_receipts` | `status=posted`, receipt_date=today, selected branch | Sum receipt amounts once |
| Spent today | `supplier_payments` + `expenses` | `status=posted`, payment_date/expense_date=today, selected branch | Sum paid supplier settlements and paid expenses; excludes bills themselves |
| Invoiced today | `sales_invoices` | `status=posted`, invoice_date=today, selected branch | Sum grand_total; issued/dated invoice activity, not cash or VAT-exclusive revenue |
| Purchases today | `purchase_bills` | `status=posted`, bill_date=today, selected branch | Sum grand_total; recorded bills, not cash outflow |
| Revenue / costs & expenses / net result MTD | `get_profit_and_loss` | posted/reversed journals, dates from month start through today, selected branch | income; COGS+expenses; authoritative ledger netProfit (not audited) |
| Comparable previous month | same P&L RPC | previous month start through same elapsed calendar day, clamped to month end | current minus previous amounts; no percent if previous=0 |
| Upcoming | same open-item reports | open/partial, positive outstanding, due today through seven days | earliest 5 due documents; overdue items stay in Attention |
| Recommendation | overdue receivable rows | genuinely overdue positive remaining | first ranked customer invoice only; review link, no outbound message |
| Business health | cash + open receivable/payable reports | only when sources are available and posted business data exists | 100 minus documented deductions below |

Attention: overdue customer invoices rank above overdue supplier bills, which rank above bills due in the next seven days. Customer overdue age ranks first within collections, with outstanding amount as a same-age tie breaker. Obligations rank by overdue vs soon-due and then materiality. At most four are rendered; the complete ranking is computed server-side. No missing-document, VAT, reconciliation, or bookkeeping warnings are inferred.

Health starts at 100 and deducts 15 for any overdue receivable, 10 more for an invoice over 30 days overdue, 10 for any overdue payable, 5 for any bill due in the next seven days, and 15 if posted cash is below next-seven-day payable amounts. Good is 80–100; Watch 60–79; Needs attention below 60. It is a deterministic indicator, not AI or an audited risk rating. On missing sources it is withheld; with no posted business data it is hidden.

The open-item and per-day document responses are limited to 1,001 rows each. If a source exceeds 1,000 rows or fails, the affected totals are withheld with an explicit warning rather than shown as a partial financial sum. Due-item reports are also withheld when their open total differs from the dashboard's exact current AR/AP total. An exact server-side aggregation beyond that scale is deferred until an approved schema/RPC design is available. The existing dashboard and P&L RPCs return exact aggregations without transferring ledger rows to the browser. Note that the existing open-item report uses *current* remaining amounts even for an `as_of` date, so this page presents current open commitments, not a historic aging reconstruction. It does not independently recheck source document/journal reversal status; if reversed-source open items remain active, this is an upstream open-item/report inconsistency that must be reconciled in QA, not masked by Today.

QA reconciliation should compare: (1) Today cash to Dashboard cash_on_hand+cash_at_bank and Cash/Bank accounts; (2) Today outstanding and overdue to Accounts Receivable open-item report for the same selected branch and date; (3) payable due items to Accounts Payable open-item report; (4) Today invoiced to posted Sales Invoices dated today; (5) daily received/spent to posted receipts/payments/paid expenses; (6) pulse to P&L with its shown period. Do not certify totals without authenticated QA access.
