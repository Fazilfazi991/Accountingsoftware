# Ask FYNTA workspace V2

Mode: Operate. Small-business owners ask grounded financial questions or begin confirmation-first guided actions inside the existing FYNTA application shell.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One calm financial work surface replaces the nested assistant application. It refuses permanent conversation rails, oversized welcome chrome, and competing scroll regions.

OWN-WORLD: Existing FYNTA light surfaces, dark ink, soft gray structure, precise one-pixel borders, and restrained blue accents. Compact controls and financial typography carry the interface; no decorative avatar or repeated logo appears in content.

STORY: The user lands on one focused question composer, scans compact popular questions and three active actions, then transitions into a readable conversation or a wide guided workflow without leaving the workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A lightweight Ask FYNTA header sits above an upper-middle empty state. The composer is the visual anchor, followed by two compact rows for popular questions and quick actions. History and New conversation live at top-right and reserve no width when closed.

FORM: User-pinned single-workspace composition, first and only direction. Seed key: user-pinned-ask-fynta-v2. History is a temporary overlay; the conversation becomes the sole interior scroll region after activation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
